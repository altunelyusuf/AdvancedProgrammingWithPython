hasRulingStatement : A lineage whose items are all Done or Cancelled and whose objectives meet their targets on their last reading is finished, and a finished lineage is concluded: its closure report states what it measured, learned and could not show, every guard it promised to hold is read again at the end, the Owner confirms the report, the mission is set Achieved or Abandoned, and the lineage is then set down into the archive in the next release. Setting a lineage down never removes what a live lineage uses. Until the comprehensive design of lineage governance, the work that governs lineages (concluding, setting down, pausing, switching, starting, and keeping the lineage metadata) is recorded in this lineage as a category of its own, Lineage governance, so that it can be counted apart from the work of delivery. The lineage whose product work is delivered is named the Delivery lineage; the name of the governing lineage is not yet chosen. The enforcement is advisory first: it is a stipulation proposed to the Owner and is not enforced against real work until the Owner rules on it.
