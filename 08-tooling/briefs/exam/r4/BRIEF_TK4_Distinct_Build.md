## The task
**Build the near-copy rule for exam items: the 4-word-sequence method, its SHACL shape and its fixtures of known answer**

Build the near-copy rule of the Owner's ruling of 2026-10-30. Method (design section 2.1 of 04-assessment/exams/exam_design_v1_0_0.md states it; this task builds it): normalise (lower case; words are runs of letters, digits and underscore); the text of an item is its stem, its code listing and its options joined; C(new, seed) is the share of the new item's 4-word sequences that also occur in the seed; an item is a near copy of a seed when C is at least 0.50 and the correct answers are equal as normalised text, or when C is at least 0.80 whatever the answer. The seeds ('page-visible set', what a student can open before the sitting) are every item of the newest question_bank_v*.json and of quiz_v1_0_0.json in 08-tooling/chNN-page for chapters 01 to 08 (all eight, chapter 5 too, because students can open it) and every listing of the newest chapter-code manifest 03-materials/chNN/page/chapter_code_chNN_v*.json; a listing that equals a seed listing after whitespace is a copy unless a change of case alters what it prints (decide by running both). The rule also applies between two papers (the make-up repeats no item of the midterm). Deliver, as NEW files: (1) 04-assessment/exams/exam_nocopy_v1_0_0.py, a library and a command line: given an items file (a JSON list of item records with id, stem, code, options, answer as in section 2 of the design) it prints per item the largest C, the seed it matches, whether the answers are equal, and the verdict, and exits 0 only when no item is a near copy; (2) 04-assessment/exams/exam_nocopy_shapes_v1_0_0.ttl, the ruling as SHACL (a shape over the RDF description of a measured item that refuses a near copy); a ruling is never kept as prose; (3) 04-assessment/exams/exam_nocopy_fixtures_v1_0_0.json and 04-assessment/exams/exam_nocopy_fixtures_check_v1_0_0.py: fixtures of known answer, namely the five variants of design section 2.1 (identical, one word changed, stem rewritten and options kept, stem rewritten and two options swapped, a new angle) with the values the design states (1.000, 0.942, 0.871, 0.786, 0.000) and their verdicts, plus a pair with C between 0.50 and 0.80 and different answers (not a copy), a pair with C of 0.80 or more and different answers (a copy), a listing copy that differs by whitespace only (a copy), a listing that differs by case and prints differently (not a copy), and a between-papers pair; the check prints expected and actual for every fixture, runs the SHACL on a conforming and a violating graph, and exits 0 only when every one is as expected.
