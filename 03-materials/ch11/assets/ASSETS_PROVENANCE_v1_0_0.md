the two portraits as the 500 px and 960 px thumbnails of the Wikimedia thumbnailer). Licence and credit of each file were read from the extmetadata of the Commons API on the day of download. Each picture was
saved as an RGB JPEG of at most 900 px on the long side (the Neumann portrait 960 px wide).

The story texts rest on the story companion 08-tooling/sen0414_ch11_stories_v1_0_0.py (Katz and Conway, public-domain press release, 14 February 1989; Gailly and Adler, zlib 0.9, 1 May 1995; Kare, Macintosh trash can, 1983;
Daley and Neumann, Multics file system, Fall Joint Computer Conference 1965). The live links of the story slides and of the resources slide were fetched on 2026-10-25: all answered HTTP 200 except the YouTube video page
(HTTP 429 to the fetch tool; the oEmbed endpoint of YouTube returned the title "Python Tutorial: Zip Files - Creating and Extracting Zip Archives" by Corey Schafer, so the video exists).
The pictures show: the PKZIP floppy (the product of the 1989 format), Mark Adler (one of the two authors of zlib; Jean-loup Gailly has no free portrait), Susan Kare, and Peter G. Neumann (co-author of the 1965 paper; R. C. Daley has no free portrait).
